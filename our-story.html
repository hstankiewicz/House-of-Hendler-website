Our Story

Built on the little things.

House of Hendler is a lifestyle brand rooted in timeless style, playful details and beautifully made everyday objects.

Where We Started

A love of beautiful, well-made things.

House of Hendler began with a simple idea: that the objects we use every day should be as thoughtfully designed as the spaces we keep them in. Bright color, classic motifs and a little bit of whimsy — that’s the House of Hendler point of view.

Our first collection, the Palm Bunnies, brought that idea to life as a set of enamel needle minders finished with gold detailing. They were made for the needlepoint table, but they’ve found homes well beyond it.

Where We’re Headed

More than a needlepoint brand.

The Palm Bunny Collection is our launch — not our identity. House of Hendler will grow to include accessories, gifts, home and lifestyle pieces, all sharing the same DNA: Palm Beach polish, chinoiserie influence and classic American preppy style, made with care.

Every future piece will carry the same standard: timeless over trendy, detail over decoration, and quality you can feel.
